  1. Photograph one leaf

Why early detection matters

Black Sigatoka is a fungal disease that destroys banana leaf tissue and cuts fruit yield. It isn't established on the Australian mainland, but it is present on outer Torres Strait islands and in every country to Australia's north.

A 2001 outbreak in north Queensland's main growing region took a multi-year eradication effort to clear. Finding it early keeps the response small.
