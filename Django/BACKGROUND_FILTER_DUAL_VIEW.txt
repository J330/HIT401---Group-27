DINOv2 STAGE-1 DUAL-VIEW BACKGROUND FILTER
=========================================

What changed
------------
Stage 1 now analyses TWO versions of every uploaded image:

1. The original uploaded image.
2. A foreground-isolated version with the background replaced by neutral grey
   and the detected object cropped with a small amount of padding.

When background removal succeeds, the Banana Leaf and Not Banana Leaf
similarity scores are combined 50/50 before the existing 65% threshold and
Banana-vs-Not-Banana margin checks are applied.

Stage 2 is unchanged. The disease classifier, reference-similarity check and
heatmap continue to use the ORIGINAL image.

No retraining is required
-------------------------
This change does not retrain DINOv2 or the disease classifier, and it does not
require rebuilding initial_references.pt. The filtered view is supplementary to
the existing reference-gate calculation.

Setup
-----
From the Django folder, install/update dependencies:

    pip install -r requirements.txt

The new dependency is rembg[cpu]. On the first scan it may download the small
pre-trained u2netp background-removal model and cache it locally. Later scans
reuse the cached model.

If background removal cannot run, the website does NOT fail the scan. It falls
back to original-image-only Stage 1 and shows that fallback on the result card.
